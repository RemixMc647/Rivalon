AboutRivalon

Rivalon is a character matchup site built to settle the biggest debates in fiction.
Who would win in a fight: Batman or Goku? Superman or a hero from your favorite anime?
Pick two characters, start the matchup, and Rivalon does the rest.

Compare. Analyze. Find the Ultimate Winner.

Rivalon isn't a coin flip. For every matchup, it looks at each character's powers, abilities, gear,
feats, and weaknesses, then breaks down how the fight could play out.
You get a full side-by-side comparison with stat bars for strength, speed, endurance, durability, flight, and more.

More than one outcome

Fights don't always end the same way. A character might win in open combat but lose with no preparation,
or win with the right gear or the right weakness to exploit. Rivalon shows the different ways a matchup can go,
so you see the whole story and not just the final result. Then it gives you a clear winner.

Every kind of character

Rivalon covers superheroes, anime characters, movie icons, and more, and the roster keeps growing.

Simple and fast

Put in Character 1, Put in Character 2, and press Start Matchup. In seconds you have a detailed breakdown and a winner.

Just for fun

Rivalon matchups are made for entertainment and friendly debate.
Results come from the powers and feats the characters show in their stories, and fans will always disagree about them. That's half the fun.
